Methodology & Assumptions

• Golden Ratio (55%): Accounts for average regional pricing dilution, 21% VAT, Valve's 30% platform cut, and baseline PC/VR refund rates (10-18%).

• Steam Next Fest Multiplier: Calculated strictly as a multiplier. 'Bronze' tier (<1k wishlists) historically yields ~462 additions. 'Silver' tier yields ~1.5k.

• Cost Modeling: Assumes a baseline minimum wage opportunity cost (€2,608/mo) + NL 2026 base living expenses (€2,037/mo). As a solo dev, working on the game actively burns both metrics.
