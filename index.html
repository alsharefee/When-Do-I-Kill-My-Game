Weeks Left To Live

Harsh Truth Time

ABORT MISSION: Your game is currently performing like a lead balloon. It's time to gracefully back away, scrub your hard drive, and update your LinkedIn profile.

  • Weeks 1-2: Delete all those ambitious ideas you had in the shower.
  • Week 3: Staple together whatever you have. Slap on some duct tape.
  • Week 4: Click "Publish" while looking away from the screen.
  • Week 5: Try not to look at the review scores. (They are mixed).
  • Week 6: Abandon ship and start a new prototype you will also abandon.

Harsh Truth Time

TREADING WATER: You might buy a few more months of instant noodles, but that's about it. Don't let this minor success give you a big head.

  • Finish whatever you're building right now and DO NOT add multiplayer.
  • Do not write a "post-launch roadmap". You are not Hello Games.
  • If wishlist velocity drops, immediately refer to the red protocol above.

Harsh Truth Time

HOLY CRAP, YOU MIGHT MAKE IT: The math says you can actually afford to keep working on this!

  • Don't let it go to your head, but maybe treat yourself to a non-ramen meal tonight.
  • Your game might actually be fun. Or the algorithm glitched. We take those.
  • Please don't blow the budget on hiring an orchestral composer.

Methodology & Assumptions

• Gaben's Toll (55% Ratio): Accounts for Valve's 30% cut, taxes, regional pricing (where your game is $2 in Argentina), and people rage-refunding your bugs.

• Steam Next Fest Slot Machine: Calculated purely on vibes and historical data. If you have under 1k wishlists, you get ~462 pity wishlists. Over 1k, the algorithm blesses you with ~1.5k.

• The Starving Artist Model: Assumes you still need to pay rent. As a solo dev, working on this game actively burns a hole in your wallet.
