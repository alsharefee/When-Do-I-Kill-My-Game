  • • The Good News: You won't starve to death making this game!
  • • The Bad News: Because you have no financial pressure, this game will likely stay in development for the next 12 years.
  • • Try to actually finish a level this weekend instead of refactoring your codebase again.

Methodology & Assumptions

Gaben's Toll (55% Ratio): Accounts for Valve's 30% cut, taxes, regional pricing (where your game is $2 in Argentina), and people rage-refunding your bugs.

Lifetime Tail (7.5x Multiplier): A standard industry estimate. Your total sales over a 5-year lifespan usually equal about 7.5x your Week 1 launch sales.

Steam Next Fest Slot Machine: Calculated purely on vibes and historical data. If you have under 1k wishlists, you get ~462 pity wishlists. Over 1k, the algorithm blesses you with ~1.5k.

The Starving Artist Model: Assumes you still need to pay rent. As a dev, working on this game actively burns a hole in your wallet.
