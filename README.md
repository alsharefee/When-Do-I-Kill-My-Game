# When Do I Kill My Game? 💀

Welcome to the most depressing (yet highly scientific) calculator on the internet. 

Are you a solo indie dev? Have you been working on your "dream game" for 3 years? Do you sometimes look at your Steam wishlists and silently weep? **This repository is for you.**

"When Do I Kill My Game" is a harsh-truth calculator designed to tell you exactly when it's time to take your beloved project out back and put it out of its misery, before you end up living in a cardboard box eating raw ramen packets.

🌐 **Try it live and face reality:** [https://alsharefee.github.io/When-Do-I-Kill-My-Game/](https://alsharefee.github.io/When-Do-I-Kill-My-Game/)

## How To Use It (To Destroy Your Dreams)

1. **Open the Calculator:** Click the link above. Take a deep breath.
2. **Set the "Copium Dials":**
   - **Delusional Wishlists:** Input how many wishlists you *actually* have (not how many your mom promised she'd get you).
   - **Price:** How much are you charging? (Be honest, no one is paying $39.99 for your first puzzle platformer).
   - **Conversion Rate:** The percentage of wishlisters who will actually open their wallets. (10% is pure hopium).
   - **Ramen Budget:** How much money you bleed every month just staying alive.
   - **Steam Next Fest:** Have you already used your magic algorithm bullet, or is it still in the chamber?
3. **Read the Verdict:** Look at the giant "Weeks Left To Live" number at the bottom. 
4. **Cry:** Optional, but highly recommended.
5. **Follow the Directives:** Depending on the color of your verdict box (Red = Abort, Amber = Treading Water, Green = Wait, really?), follow the harsh truth bullet points to plan your next life choices.
